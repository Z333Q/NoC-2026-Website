## Send a Message

The page has a contact form with these fields:

- Name (name, text, required). Placeholder: "Your name"
- Email (email, email address, required). Placeholder: "your@email.com"
- Company (company, text, optional). Placeholder: "Your company"
- Topic (topic, select, optional). Default: "Select a topic". Options:
  - Investment Inquiry
  - Technical Partnership
  - P402 Integration
  - Advisory
  - Academic / Research
  - Media & Press
  - General
- Message (message, multi-line text, required). Placeholder: "What would you like to discuss?"

Submit button: "Send Message"

On success: "Message Sent. Thank you for reaching out. We will review your message and get back to you shortly."

On error: "There was an error sending your message. Please try again."

The form only works in the browser with JavaScript. If you can't use it, email zeshan@natureofcommerce.com instead.
